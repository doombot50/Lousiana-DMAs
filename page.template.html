Method & sources

Where this comes from

Dollar figures are computed by build_station_spend.py in this repo, which reads {{rows_m}} expenditure rows from the Louisiana Ethics Administration filings (via the campaign-finance project's .la_cache) and sums what every committee paid each station. Three corrections matter:

Spelling variants. One station is filed 15–30 ways — KADN, KADN-TV, KADN - TV, KADN-FOX, KADN-TV CHANNEL 15. All fold to one call sign.

Radio sisters. WWL and KTAL share their call signs with radio stations. Rows explicitly marked radio (WWL-AM, WWL RADIO, KTAL-FM) are excluded — in total. Rows carrying only the bare call sign cannot be resolved either way; they are counted as television, and that amount — , nearly all of it WWL — is disclosed here rather than buried. Treat WWL-TV's and KTAL's totals as ceilings.

Combined buys. sits in rows naming two co-owned stations at once (KARD-TV KTVE-TV, WGMB WVLA-TV); those amounts are split evenly rather than assigned to whichever sorts first.

One gap: the {{gap_year}} expenditure file is absent from the upstream data release, so {{gap_year}} is missing from every figure on this page. It was an off-cycle year — the off-years either side drew {{gap_near}} — so the effect on the totals is small, but it is not zero.

Parish boundaries are U.S. Census cartographic files; parish-to-DMA assignment was computed by point-in-polygon against Nielsen DMA boundaries and cross-checked against published market lists. Ownership is as reported through September 2026.

Notes on each section: how each chart's figures are counted

Party labels. Party is the filing's own label, except that Louisiana files most PACs and some candidates as OTH, which says nothing about which side bought the airtime. Where a filer's alignment is public record the label is corrected — the RGA's PAC to Republican; Gumbo PAC, Walter Boasso and Buddy Leach to Democratic; Jay Dardenne to Republican. A filing that states a party is left alone unless public reporting shows it is wrong: Blair Downing Edwards, a Republican 2026 Supreme Court candidate whose committee files as Democratic, is shown as Republican. Every correction is listed in filings.py.
