Pick a race, mark who won or who made the runoff, add vote shares if you have them, and share the card. It shows what each candidate raised, spent and put on the air, and whether the bigger spender won. The page holds no results: the call is yours.

State filings through __THROUGH__

    What a card shows. Raised and spent are each candidate's totals for the {{cycle}} cycle: state candidates from their Louisiana finance reports, via the campaign-finance dashboard, and federal candidates from the FEC's summary of House and Senate campaigns for the two-year cycle. On the air is the dollars stations' FCC political files show booked for the candidate, read from the orders themselves, where at least half the candidate's orders give a figure; otherwise what the candidate reported paying TV and radio stations this cycle; and where neither has a dollar figure, the number of ad orders stations have posted. It is the same matching as the races page, where each figure is explained in full. A candidate is matched to a committee by name, only when exactly one fits; check a surprising figure there before posting.

    The bigger spender. Compares the top two candidates by money spent. How often a leader with that edge wins is from the campaign-finance dashboard's study of every contested Louisiana race since 2000. Calls you mark are kept in this browser only, so the list can show which races you've done.
